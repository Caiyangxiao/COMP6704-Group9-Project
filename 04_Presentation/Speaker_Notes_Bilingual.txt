SLIDE 05 | The Same Lasso Objective for Every Solver

ENGLISH SCRIPT
The objective has two terms. The squared loss measures training fit, and the L one penalty encourages sparse coefficients. We use the normalization one over two n everywhere. This matters because changing that normalization also changes the gradient and the effective penalty scale. X contains training features, y is the centered response, and w contains the coefficients. SLIDE 07 | Convexity and Sparse Optimality
Speaker: CAI Yangxiao 26023041R
Suggested time: 75 seconds | 04:30–05:45 | Main presentation: 30:00
English and Chinese are alternative scripts; present in one language.

ENGLISH SCRIPT
The least-squares Hessian is X transpose X divided by n, which is positive semidefinite. The L one norm is also convex, so the full objective is convex. With a positive lambda, the objective grows when the coefficient norm grows without bound, ensuring a minimizer exists. Full column rank is sufficient for a unique coefficient vector, although uniqueness can also hold in other cases. The optimality condition balances the loss gradient against a penalty subgradient. For a nonzero coefficient, the gradient must equal minus lambda times its sign. At zero, the absolute-value subgradient is the interval from minus one to one, so a gradient magnitude below lambda is compatible with optimality. These conditions apply jointly to the complete vector. The constrained formulation provides an equivalent geometric view at a corresponding norm budget. They explain exact zeros, but they do not establish correct support recovery.

中文讲解稿
最小二乘损失的 Hessian 是 X 转置乘 X 再除以 n，因此半正定。L1 范数也是凸函数，所以整个目标是凸的。当 lambda 为正时，系数范数无限增长会使目标无限增长，因此最优解存在。满列秩足以保证系数唯一，但并不是唯一性的必要条件。最优性条件要求损失梯度与惩罚次梯度相互平衡。对于非零系数，梯度必须等于负 lambda 乘其符号；在零点，绝对值次梯度包含负一到一的整个区间，因此梯度绝对值不超过 lambda 就可以满足最优性。条件必须对整个向量共同成立。约束形式还可以在对应范数预算下提供等价的几何视角。它解释精确零，但不能证明找回了真实支持集。

SLIDE 08 | Soft Thresholding
Speaker: CAI Yangxiao 26023041R
Suggested time: 65 seconds | 05:45–06:50 | Main presentation: 30:00
English and Chinese are alternative scripts; present in one language.

ENGLISH SCRIPT
Soft thresholding turns the subgradient argument into a concrete operation. It subtracts the threshold from the input magnitude and clips negative results to zero, while retaining the sign of the remaining values. With threshold zero point five, one point two becomes zero point seven, minus zero point eight becomes minus zero point three, and zero point two becomes zero. The flat middle segment therefore creates exact sparsity. This operation solves a separable proximal problem: remain near the input while paying an absolute-value penalty. For appropriately normalized orthogonal features, it gives the full Lasso solution in one step. General predictors interact, so thresholding becomes a repeated building block. All four methods will use it, but at different points in their updates.

中文讲解稿
软阈值把次梯度条件变成具体操作：从输入的绝对值减去阈值，把负结果截断为零，并保留其余数值的符号。阈值为零点五时，一点二变成零点七，负零点八变成负零点三，零点二变成零。因此图中间的水平段产生精确稀疏性。它求解一个可分离的近端问题：尽量接近输入，同时支付绝对值惩罚。对于适当归一化的正交特征，一次软阈值就能给出完整 Lasso 解；一般特征会相互作用，所以阈值成为反复使用的基本步骤。四种算法都会用到它，但使用的位置不同。

SLIDE 11 | ISTA: Gradient Step, Then Threshold
Speaker: XUE Pengyu 26022951R
Suggested time: 70 seconds | 07:40–08:50 | Main presentation: 30:00
English and Chinese are alternative scripts; present in one language.

ENGLISH SCRIPT
ISTA begins at the current coefficient vector. A gradient step reduces the smooth squared loss, and soft thresholding then handles the nonsmooth penalty exactly. The gradient is Lipschitz continuous with constant L, the squared spectral norm of X divided by n. We use the reciprocal of L as the step size. This gives a quadratic upper bound on the loss, whose minimizer together with the penalty is the displayed update. With a valid step, the objective is nonincreasing. For dense data, the dominant operations are a prediction and a transposed matrix-vector product, so one round costs order n d. The code does not store a full Gram matrix. We use the same initialization for every method. ISTA's appeal is its simple structure and predictable progress, but weak-curvature directions can require many rounds.

中文讲解稿
ISTA 从当前系数向量开始。先用梯度步降低光滑平方损失，再通过软阈值精确处理非光滑惩罚。梯度的 Lipschitz 常数 L 等于 X 的谱范数平方除以 n，我们采用它的倒数作为步长。这样可以构造损失的二次上界；把上界与惩罚一起最小化，就得到展示的更新。有效步长下，目标值不会增加。对于稠密数据，主要操作是预测和转置矩阵向量乘法，因此每轮成本为 n d 的量级。代码不存储完整 Gram 矩阵。所有方法使用相同初始点。ISTA 的优点是结构简单、进展较平稳，但弱曲率方向可能需要很多轮。

SLIDE 12 | FISTA: Add Extrapolation
Speaker: XUE Pengyu 26022951R
Suggested time: 80 seconds | 08:50–10:10 | Main presentation: 30:00
English and Chinese are alternative scripts; present in one language.

ENGLISH SCRIPT
FISTA uses the same gradient and thresholding step, but evaluates it at an extrapolated vector. The extrapolation combines the current solution with the direction from the previous solution, weighted by the momentum sequence. We initialize the momentum at one and apply the standard recursion. The dominant matrix-vector products remain similar to ISTA, while extra vectors and arithmetic maintain the momentum. Under the stated convexity and smoothness assumptions, ISTA has an order one over k objective-error bound and FISTA has an order one over k squared bound. These are worst-case bounds on objective error. They do not directly bound our stationarity residual, coefficient error, or wall-clock time. Standard FISTA can also be nonmonotone. We deliberately use the standard algorithm without restart, so the method being compared is clear. A favorable theoretical bound is therefore a reason to test acceleration, rather than a measured timing result. The results will show why this distinction matters.

中文讲解稿
FISTA 使用相同的梯度和阈值步骤，但计算位置变成一个外推向量。外推把当前解与前后两次解的移动方向结合，权重由动量序列控制。我们把动量初始化为一，采用标准递推。主要矩阵向量乘法与 ISTA 类似，但需要额外向量和运算维护动量。在给定的凸性与光滑性条件下，ISTA 的目标误差界是 k 分之一量级，FISTA 则是 k 平方分之一。这些是目标误差的最坏情况界，并不直接约束我们的平稳性残差、系数误差或者实际时间。标准 FISTA 的进展还可能非单调。因此，有利理论界是检验加速效果的理由，还需要实际计时验证。我们不使用重启，保证比较对象明确；之后会看到这种区别为什么重要。

SLIDE 13 | Cyclic Coordinate Descent
Speaker: XUE Pengyu 26022951R
Suggested time: 75 seconds | 10:10–11:25 | Main presentation: 30:00
English and Chinese are alternative scripts; present in one language.

ENGLISH SCRIPT
Coordinate descent fixes every coefficient except one and minimizes the resulting scalar Lasso problem exactly. The partial residual removes the contribution of the current coordinate from the fitted response. Thresholding the feature-residual inner product, then dividing by the feature's squared norm, gives the new coefficient. Standardization makes that denominator one, but our code retains the general formula. We maintain the residual throughout the sweep, adding back the old coordinate contribution and subtracting the new one after each update. This avoids recomputing a full prediction for every coefficient. One coordinate costs order n, and a complete sweep costs order n d. Exact scalar minimization makes the objective nonincreasing. However, a sweep contains d sequential updates, implemented here in a Python loop. Our comparison does not transfer randomized-coordinate rates to this cyclic implementation. A small sweep count must therefore be interpreted alongside elapsed time.

中文讲解稿
坐标下降固定除一个系数以外的所有系数，精确最小化对应的标量 Lasso 问题。部分残差先从拟合中移除当前坐标的贡献。对特征与残差的内积做阈值，再除以该特征的平方范数，就得到新系数。标准化使分母为一，但代码保留通用公式。整个扫描过程中持续维护残差：先加回旧坐标贡献，更新后再减去新贡献，避免每个系数都重新计算完整预测。单个坐标成本是 n 的量级，一整轮是 n d。精确最小化使目标非增。我们不会把随机坐标算法的速率直接用于这个循环实现。不过，一轮包含 d 次顺序更新，而且这里使用 Python 循环，因此较少轮数必须结合实际耗时理解。

SLIDE 14 | ADMM: Split the Loss and Penalty
Speaker: XUE Pengyu 26022951R
Suggested time: 90 seconds | 11:25–12:55 | Main presentation: 30:00
English and Chinese are alternative scripts; present in one language.

ENGLISH SCRIPT
ADMM introduces two coefficient copies. The squared loss uses x, the L one penalty uses z, and the equality constraint requires them to agree. The augmented Lagrangian adds a quadratic agreement penalty with positive parameter beta and a scaled dual vector u. Each iteration first solves a regularized least-squares system for x. It then applies soft thresholding to x plus u, with threshold lambda divided by beta, to obtain z. Finally, the dual update accumulates the disagreement between the two copies. All three vectors start at zero. We use beta for the algorithmic penalty because rho already denotes feature correlation in the experiments. Changing beta changes the iteration, while changing lambda changes the statistical model. When the split constraint holds, the formulation is exactly the original Lasso problem. The extra dual state records agreement progress between iterations, while leaving the training inputs unchanged. Convex ADMM theory supports convergence with fixed positive beta and exact subproblem solves, but does not promise that ADMM will beat every other method in finite time.

中文讲解稿
ADMM 引入两个系数副本：平方损失使用 x，L1 惩罚使用 z，并要求 x 与 z 相等。增广拉格朗日加入正参数 beta 控制的二次一致性惩罚，以及缩放对偶变量 u。每轮先通过一个正则化最小二乘线性系统更新 x，再对 x 加 u 做阈值为 lambda 除以 beta 的软阈值，得到 z；最后把两个副本的差异累加到对偶变量。三个向量都从零开始。我们用 beta 表示算法参数，因为 rho 已用于实验中的特征相关性。改变 beta 会改变迭代过程，改变 lambda 才会改变统计模型。约束满足时，这就是原来的 Lasso 问题。额外对偶状态记录副本一致性的迭代进展，训练输入仍然相同。固定正 beta、精确子问题求解下，凸 ADMM 理论支持收敛，但不保证有限时间内优于所有方法。

SLIDE 16 | Algorithm Trade-offs and Fair Comparison
Speaker: XUE Pengyu 26022951R
Suggested time: 50 seconds | 13:50–14:40 | Main presentation: 30:00
English and Chinese are alternative scripts; present in one language.

ENGLISH SCRIPT
All four methods perform different work within a round. ISTA and FISTA mainly use matrix-vector products. A CD sweep also has order n d work, but visits every coordinate sequentially. Dense ADMM first constructs and factors a d by d system, giving a larger preparation cost and extra quadratic storage. Its monitored rounds combine triangular solves with evaluation of the original objective and residual. Reusing a factor within one solve helps, but we do not reuse it across cold solves. SLIDE 29 | Conclusions and Limits
Speaker: ZHOU Xiyu 26025265R
Suggested time: 75 seconds | 28:15–29:30 | Main presentation: 30:00

ENGLISH SCRIPT
The main conclusion is conditional rather than a universal ranking. All four implementations reach nearly identical Lasso solutions. ISTA has the lowest mean time on our tested independent-feature designs. ADMM is fastest on the saved Diabetes split and has the lowest mean time at positive block correlations, but FISTA wins on two of the three most-correlated instances. SLIDE 32 | Backup — ADMM Residual Diagnostics

Q&A / backup: outside the 30-minute main presentation.

ENGLISH SCRIPT
Use this backup only if asked about ADMM feasibility or stopping. The primal residual measures disagreement between x and z; the dual residual measures the beta-scaled change in z.
